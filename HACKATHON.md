- Technical Implementation is 30%, so ship an actual ML component (trained or fine-tuned model, honest evaluation), not a prompt wrapper.
- Sponsor Featherless AI (open-weight inference) is a natural *optional* backend; on-device inference keeps the cold demo keyless.

**Suggested lane:** Applied ML outside health, school and law: accessibility, environment, creative tools, local community.
