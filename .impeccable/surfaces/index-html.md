# Surface brief: Brackets app (index.html)

**Scope & mode:** the whole single-page site, a tool first, explainer second. Mode: **Operate** (the visitor captions a file); the explainer sections below the tool read as **Read**.
**Audience & job:** a creator/teacher at a laptop, finishing a video, wanting DHH-accessible sound captions; hackathon judges on a laptop, often cold. Primary action: **Try the sample** (zero setup), then **Open a file**. Proof: the captions landing on a real soundtrack, and the held-out numbers.
**Constraints:** static, keyless, self-hosted model files; WCAG 2.2 AA; 375 px; no information by sound alone; reduced motion.
**Unresolved:** final sample clip (must be license-clean and never tuned on).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A screenplay is where sound is first written down ("A door SLAMS."). Brackets writes the soundtrack back into words, so the tool *is* a shooting-script page that types itself as it listens. It refuses the category default: a gradient "AI caption generator" hero with an upload card, or a dark editor with neon waveforms.

OWN-WORLD: white 20-lb script pages with two brass brads, on a drenched pale revision-blue ground. Courier Prime only (regular/bold/italic), 12-pt script metrics, WGA revision colors (blue, pink, yellow, green, goldenrod) as state colors, margin asterisks for human-revised lines, timecodes in the margins where scene numbers sit, and a CEA-608 caption box (white Courier on opaque black) wherever captions sit on video.

STORY: the visitor sees a muted clip caption itself, believes it because every line shows its confidence and moment, edits or strikes lines like a script revision, and exports the "revision" as a .vtt/.srt, then reads coverage (the evaluation) and the model card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left 7/12 is the video, captions in 608 boxes, with a sound strip under it; right 5/12 is script page 1 ("FADE IN:" / slug "INT. YOUR SOUNDTRACK — CONTINUOUS"), where cue lines type in with margin timecodes. The one-line thesis goes above in 12-pt caps. The primary action "Try the sample" sits on the page; "Open a file" is beside it.

FORM: screenplay / shooting-script format, position 4 of my ordered list (608 caption box, spotting sheet, SRT grammar, **screenplay**, comic SFX lettering, Teletext 888, graphic notation). Seed key e70982b7 (degraded roll: no challengers).
